Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I register?

Go to the Register page, fill in your details and submit the form.

2. How do I log in?

Use your Student ID and Password on the Login page.

3. Where can I see college events?

Visit the Events page to view all upcoming events.

4. Can I update my profile?

Yes, you can update your information from the Profile page.

5. How do I contact the administrator?

You can send your suggestions using the Feedback page.
