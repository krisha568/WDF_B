About Student Hub

StudentHub is a basic student portal developed using HTML5. It provides information about students, registration, events, and academic activities.

Mission

To provide students with an easy platform to access important college information.

Vision

To improve communication between students and the institution through a simple online portal.

Objectives

  • Easy Student Registration
  • Display College Events
  • Provide Student Information
  • Collect Student Feedback
