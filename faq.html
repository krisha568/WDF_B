Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I register on the Student Hub Portal?

Click on the Register page, fill in the required details, and submit the form.

2. How do I log in to my account?

Open the Login page, enter your username and password, and click Login.

3. How can I update my profile?

Go to the Profile page and use the available options to edit your information.

4. How do I register for events?

Visit the Events page and select the event you want to join.

5. How can I submit feedback?

Open the Feedback page, enter your comments, and submit the form.
